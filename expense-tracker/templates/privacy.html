Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects personal financial information that you voluntarily provide when using our service. This includes expense entries, category selections, amounts, dates, and descriptions. We also collect account information such as your email address and authentication credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for the purpose of providing and improving our expense tracking services. Your financial information is processed to display expense summaries, generate reports, and help you understand your spending patterns. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your expense data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. While we strive to protect your data, please understand that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or functionality. These third parties have access to your data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your information. We do not share your financial data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may export your expense data or request complete account deletion. To exercise these rights, please contact us through the provided channels.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us. We are committed to addressing your concerns and ensuring you understand how we handle your information.
